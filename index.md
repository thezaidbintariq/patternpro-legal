# PatternPro Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2 August 2026

PatternPro ("the app") is provided by Zaid Bin Tariq ("we", "us"). This policy explains
what the app does with your data. It is written to match what the software actually does.

## Summary

- **An account is optional.** Analysing targets, saving history and correcting hits all
  work signed out, and we do not know who you are unless you choose to sign in.
- Your saved patterns, photos and analysis live **on your device**.
- A target photo is **sent to our server when your device cannot analyse it locally**.
- If you agreed when accepting the Terms, those photos are **kept and used to improve the
  detection model**. This is optional, is off unless you tick it, and you can withdraw it.
- **If you sign in, your saved patterns and target photos are backed up to your
  account** so they survive a lost phone and follow you to a second device. Signed
  out, nothing leaves the device except what is described above.
- We collect **anonymous usage events** to understand whether the app is useful.
- We **do not sell personal data**, and we use no advertising or third-party analytics SDK.

## 0. Accounts

You can use PatternPro without an account. Signing in is needed only for AI Insights
credits, for keeping Pro access across a reinstall or a second device, and for cloud sync.

If you sign in we process your **email address** and a **user id** issued by our
authentication provider (Supabase). We use them to identify your account and nothing else.
We do not use your email for marketing unless you separately opt in.

Signing in links your purchase record to your account so Pro follows you. Signing out
unlinks it on this device.

**Legal basis (UK/EU):** performance of a contract, since these features cannot work
without an identity.

## 0a. AI Insights

AI Insights is optional, and nothing else in the app depends on it. If you never open it,
no part of this section applies to you.

When you send a message, two things leave your device: **your typed question** and a
**short computed summary** of your saved targets. That summary is generated on your device
and is deliberately limited to figures, for example "23 sessions, average 61%, largest gap
5.1in, point of impact 4.2in high-right". You can see exactly what it contains, because it
is the same set of numbers the Insights screen shows you.

What is **never** sent: your target photographs, the notes you type against a session, your
email address, your account id, your device identifiers, or your location.

Your question and that summary are sent to our own server, which forwards them to
**OpenRouter** and the model provider it routes to. We do not use them to train any model,
and our providers are contractually bound not to train on them either.

Your conversation is **saved to your account** so it is still there when you come back and
on your other devices. You can clear it at any time, signing out removes the copy held on
the device, and deleting your account deletes the conversation with it. We keep the message
count separately, to meter the monthly allowance.

Answers are generated by a language model and can be wrong, including about your own
figures. They are not advice about whether a shot is safe, legal or ethical, and the
responsibility for that judgement stays with you.

## 0b. Cloud backup of your patterns

If you are signed in, the app keeps a copy of your saved sessions in your account: the
measurements, the hit coordinates, the load and choke you recorded, your notes, and the
target photograph itself. The copy is yours alone. Row Level Security in our database and
per-user rules on file storage mean no other account can read it, and we do not read it to
build profiles or to train anything.

**Signed out, none of this happens.** Analysis, history and export stay entirely on the
device, exactly as before, and the app remains fully usable that way.

Two deliberate limits on how it behaves:

- **Photographs upload only on an unmetered connection** such as wifi. A season of targets
  is hundreds of megabytes and spending your mobile data unasked would not be reasonable.
- **The device stays the working copy.** Analysis runs on-device so the app works with no
  signal; the cloud is a backup and a way to reach a second device, never a requirement.

Deleting a session deletes it from your account too. The record is kept briefly as a marker
so the deletion also reaches your other devices, then removed. Deleting your account removes
every pattern, photograph and message it holds.

## 1. What the app stores on your device

Saved patterns, hit coordinates, load and choke details, distances, and your analysis
history are written to local storage on your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

Signed out, they stay there and are not transmitted anywhere. Signed in, a copy is also
kept in your account as described in section 0b, so uninstalling no longer loses them.

## 2. Target photographs

PatternPro counts pellet holes in a photograph of a paper target.

**On-device analysis.** Where your device supports it, counting runs entirely on the device,
so you get a result with no network at all. If you have consented to retention, the
photograph is additionally uploaded in the background afterwards, purely as training data.
That upload waits for an unmetered connection such as wifi, so it never spends your mobile
data, and it is discarded rather than sent if you withdraw consent first.

**Server analysis.** Where on-device analysis is unavailable or fails, the photograph is
uploaded over HTTPS to our detection service so it can be counted, and the hit coordinates
are returned to your app.

**Retention for model improvement.** When you accept the Terms you are asked, as a separate
and optional choice, whether we may keep the target photos you analyse in order to improve
the detection model. The box is **not ticked by default**.

- If you **agree**, photographs analysed on our server are stored, together with the shot
  details you entered (distance, choke, shot size, payload), and used as training data.
- If you **decline**, the photograph is analysed and not retained.
- Declining does not restrict the app. Every feature works either way. We ask this way
  because consent that is a condition of using the software would not be freely given.

You can withdraw consent at any time; it applies to analyses from that point onward.

**Contributed photos.** Tapping **Contribute** additionally sends your *corrected* hit
coordinates, which are far more valuable as training data than the model's own guesses.

Photographs of paper targets are not intended to contain people or identifying information.
Please do not analyse an image showing anything you would not want retained if you have
consented to retention. Stored photographs carry no identifier linking them to you, which
also means we cannot single one out to delete on request (see section 7).

**Legal basis (UK/EU):** legitimate interest for server-side counting, which is necessary to
provide the feature you asked for; **consent** for retaining photographs as training data,
given by the separate optional tick box on the Terms screen.

## 3. Usage analytics

The app records anonymous product events, for example: the app was opened, an analysis was
started, an analysis was saved, how many detected hits were corrected, a price tier was
tapped, onboarding finished.

Each event carries a randomly generated installation identifier, the platform name, and the
app version. The identifier is created on your device, is not derived from any hardware or
advertising ID, cannot be linked to you, and is destroyed when you uninstall the app.

Events are queued locally and sent in batches to our own server. **We do not use a
third-party analytics or advertising SDK**, and no data is shared with data brokers or ad
networks.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise or coarse
location, or any firearm identifier such as a serial number.

**Legal basis (UK/EU):** legitimate interest in understanding whether the app works and is
used, on the basis that the data is anonymous and low-risk.

## 4. Crash reports

If the app crashes, a diagnostic report is sent to Sentry so the fault can be fixed. Reports
are configured with personally identifying information disabled. Before a report is sent, we
strip user identity, device name, and any local file paths (such as photo locations), which
could otherwise reveal album or file names.

## 5. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. We never see or
handle your card details. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous customer identifier and the
purchase state needed to unlock paid features.

## 7. Retention

- On-device data: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
- Server-side target images, where you have **not** consented to retention: not retained.
- Images queued on your device awaiting background upload: held until sent, dropped
  immediately if you withdraw consent.
- Server-side target images, where you **have** consented: retained indefinitely as model
  training data, along with the shot details you entered.
- Contributed corrections and labels: retained indefinitely as model training data.
- Anonymous usage events: retained in aggregate.
- Crash reports: per Sentry's retention, typically 90 days.
- Account record: until you delete your account.

Because stored images and analytics events carry no identifier that links back to you,
we genuinely cannot locate "your" records in order to export or erase them individually.
This is a deliberate privacy trade-off: we hold less about you, but we also cannot tie
anything to you on request.

## 8. Your rights

If you are in the UK or EEA you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction,
objection and portability under the UK GDPR / GDPR. Given the above, the practical routes
are:

- **Stop all analytics:** turn analytics off in the app, or uninstall it.
- **Stop photo retention:** withdraw training-data consent. Analyses from that point on are
  counted and not kept.
- **Erase everything held on device:** uninstall the app.
- **Delete your account:** contact us and we will remove the account record. Note this does
  not reach anonymous contributions, which carry no link to you.

To ask a question or make a request, contact zaidkhawaja21.zz@gmail.com. You may also complain to
the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
