# Delete your PatternPro account

**App:** PatternPro (`com.patternpro.app`)
**Developer:** Zaid Bin Tariq

This page explains how to have your PatternPro account and its data deleted, and what
happens to each kind of data when you do.

## The quickest way: delete it in the app

Open PatternPro, go to **Account**, and tap **Delete account**. You will be asked to
confirm, and the deletion happens immediately — no email, no waiting, no need to read the
rest of this page.

## By email instead

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, send an email to
**zaidkhawaja21.zz@gmail.com** with:

1. The subject line **Delete my PatternPro account**
2. The email address you signed in with (this is the only identifier we hold for you)

Send it from that same address if you can — it is how we confirm the request is yours.
Requests are actioned within **30 days**, and we reply to confirm once it is done. There is
no charge, and you do not need to give a reason.

## What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes, from our servers:

- your account record, including your email address and user ID
- every saved pattern session synced to your account — the measurements, setup details
  (choke, shot size, payload, distance) and any notes
- every target photograph backed up to your account
- your AI Insights conversation history
- your monthly AI message counter

## What is kept, and why

- **Target photographs you consented to donate for model training.** These are stored
  without any identifier linking them to you or your account, so once submitted they
  cannot be located and singled out for deletion. You can stop future donations at any
  time by withdrawing training-data consent on the Terms screen in the app. This consent
  is off unless you switch it on.
- **Anonymous product analytics and crash reports.** These are tied to a random install
  identifier, never to your account, and are deleted when you uninstall the app. They
  contain no personal information.
- **Purchase records held by Google Play.** Any subscription or purchase you made is
  recorded by Google Play, not by us. Manage or cancel it at
  [play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions).

## Deleting only part of your data

You do not have to delete your account to delete your data:

- **A single session:** long-press it in **History** and confirm. It is removed from your
  account and from your other devices, not just from the phone in your hand.
- **Everything on this device:** uninstall the app.
- **Turn off analytics:** Settings, then switch analytics off.

## Retention after deletion

Once processed, account data is removed from the live database immediately. Encrypted
infrastructure backups roll off within **30 days**, after which no copy remains.
